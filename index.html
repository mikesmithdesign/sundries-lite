Two small rooms off the high street.

Sundries started as a Saturday stall on Merebridge market. Now it is two rooms through the arch beside Pell's bakery, run by Frances, stocked with things she uses at home herself.

Most of it is made within a day's drive, by people we can ring when something goes wrong.
